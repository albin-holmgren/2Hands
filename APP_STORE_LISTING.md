## Description (4000 characters max)

**2Hands is Hands on AI that never sleeps.**

Delegate any computer-based task to intelligent AI agents that work around the clock. From email management to data research, 2Hands' AI agents handle the repetitive work so you can focus on what matters.

**🤖 INTELLIGENT AI AGENTS**
Create AI agents that understand your tasks in natural language. Simply describe what you need done, and 2Hands builds a custom agent to handle it—no coding required.

• Automate email inbox management
• Research competitors and market trends
• Generate weekly reports from multiple data sources
• Schedule and manage social media posts
• Extract and process data from documents

**⚡ WORKS WHILE YOU SLEEP**
Set schedules for your agents to run automatically. Daily reports at 8 AM? Weekly competitor analysis every Monday? Your agents handle it consistently, on time, every time.

**🔒 SECURE & PRIVATE**
Your credentials are encrypted and stored securely. Sensitive actions require your approval before execution. You stay in control while your agents do the heavy lifting.

**💬 NATURAL CONVERSATION**
Talk to your AI Manager just like you would a human assistant. Ask questions, assign tasks, and get real-time progress updates—all through a simple chat interface.

**📊 TRANSPARENT PROGRESS**
Watch your agents work in real-time. See exactly what they're doing, review their outputs, and approve important actions before they're executed.

**PRICING**
• Free: 300 credits daily, 2 agents
• Starter ($25/mo): 4,000 credits, 5 agents
• Pro ($40/mo): 12,000 credits, 15 agents
• Business ($100/mo): 30,000 credits, 50 agents

**GET STARTED**
Sign up in seconds and start delegating tasks to your helping hands today. Join thousands of professionals who are reclaiming their time with 2Hands.
